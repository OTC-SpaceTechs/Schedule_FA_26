    Every reading the flight computer logged, once per second for about 5 hours (including time on the ground before launch and after landing). time_s counts from when the logger powered on; launch is at .

    Acceleration and rotation on this page are the peak reading in each second (accel_peak, gyro_peak_dps). The log has no altitude column, so altitude is calculated from our pressure readings: each reading is matched to the height where the National Weather Service balloon from Springfield measured the same pressure (station 72440, 12 UTC Oct 4 (opens in new tab)). Altitudes are above sea level; the launch site sits at about ft. Our BME280 sensor is rated down to 300 hPa (about 30,000 ft), so readings above that are outside its spec.
